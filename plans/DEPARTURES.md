Good question — "forced" was too strong for most of them. Sorting them by what actually blocks each:

**First, one correction.** The `(current-display)` gate isn't really a departure. Emacs's reset runs *per frame*, and `face-spec-recalc` is only reached from `(dolist (frame (frame-list)) …)`. With no frame there is nothing to reset, so our gate is the frame loop in disguise. The genuine departure it stands for is the one below.

| **One attribute set** — no per-frame realization, no `face--new-frame-defaults`, no `internal-merge-in-global-face`; the FRAME argument everywhere is ignored      | Yes, but it's a subsystem | `frame`'s `face_alist` + face cache, real `realize_face` per frame, `face-attribute`'s FRAME argument becoming meaningful, and every reader (xdisp's face lookup, the renderers) told which frame it is asking about. Order of 400–600 lines across `faces.scm`, `frame.scm`, `xdisp.scm`, both front ends. This is the structural one, and it also dissolves `custom--frame-color-default`'s skipped test (FRAME `t` = "defaults for new frames") for free. |

So: the minibuffer-prompt one is the best value — it's a real `read_minibuf` structure and removes several of the things I had to write down. `next-line-add-newlines` is nearly free and usually rides along with it. The reverse parameter is small but low-value; per-frame faces is a planned piece of its own, not a patch.
